# Pre-publication checklist

- [ ] Confirm you own or have permission to publish the research notebook, written report, and generated figures.
- [ ] Review the DOCX for author names, institutional affiliations, and any client-specific content before publication.
- [ ] Confirm that no credentials, tokens, participant-level personally identifying information, or restricted datasets are included.
- [ ] The dataset is linked from Zenodo, not copied into the repository. Review its license and attribution.
- [ ] State the exploratory, non-clinical nature of the results and preserve negative calibration findings.
- [ ] Do not claim performance is clinical diagnostic accuracy or that the study was independently reproduced by GitHub CI.
- [ ] Upload extracted files, not the ZIP file itself.
